Antoni Gaudí

Information

Antoni Gaudí was a pioneering Catalan architect and the chief exponent of Catalan Modernisme.

Antoni Gaudí is famous as the visionary architect who shaped the skyline of Barcelona through organic, nature-inspired forms and innovative structural engineering.

Antoni Gaudí famously avoided straight lines, believing they did not exist in nature. He used complex 3D scale models with hanging weights instead of flat blueprints to engineer his masterworks, including the legendary Sagrada Família. Tragically, due to his modest and unkempt appearance later in life, he was mistaken for a beggar when struck by a tram in 1926, which delayed his lifesaving care.
